White Bird of Paradise

Strelitzia nicolai
LightBright, some direct
WaterLet top 2" dry
HumidityAverage is fine
FeedMonthly, spring–summer

01 Light

Give it as much bright light as the room allows — this plant is happiest with a few hours of direct sun, especially gentle morning sun. In low light it'll survive but stop putting out new leaves and get leggy, so a spot right by the biggest window in the room is the move.

02 Water

Water thoroughly when the top two inches of soil are dry — roughly once a week in summer, less often in winter. It's fairly drought-tolerant once established and would much rather be underwatered than sitting in soggy soil.

03 Humidity & temperature

Average indoor humidity is perfectly fine, though it won't say no to more. Keep it between 65–85°F (18–29°C) and protect it from cold drafts or temperatures below 50°F (10°C).

04 Feeding

Feed monthly with a balanced fertilizer during spring and summer to support its size and new growth. Hold off in fall and winter.

05 Common issues

  • Splitting leaves — this is completely natural, not damage. It's how the plant sheds wind resistance outdoors.
  • Brown, crispy tips — usually underwatering, dry air, or fertilizer salt buildup.
  • Yellowing leaves — most often a sign of overwatering or poor drainage.
Gift note This one wants to grow big, so don't rush a pot upgrade — it actually prefers being a little snug at the roots. Rotate it a quarter turn every so often so it grows evenly toward the light.
