Scindapsus Pictus

"Satin Pothos"
LightBright and indirect
WaterAvoid overwatering
HumidityAverage is fine
FeedMonthly, spring to fall

01 Light

Medium to bright indirect light suits it best. It'll tolerate lower light, but the silvery speckling on the leaves gets much more pronounced with brighter light.

02 Water

Let the top 1–2 inches of soil dry out between waterings. It's drought-tolerant and much more likely to run into trouble from overwatering than underwatering. When in doubt, wait a few more days.

03 Humidity & temperature

Average home humidity is fine, around 40-50%. Normal indoor temperatures, roughly 65–80°F (18–27°C), are fine but keep it away from cold windowsills in winter.

04 Feeding

A balanced liquid fertilizer once a month from spring to fall. It doesn't need feeding in the winter.

05 Common issues

  • Yellow leaves: almost always overwatering; let it dry out more between waterings.
  • Long, bare vines: a sign it needs more light, or just needs a trim to encourage fuller growth.
  • Fading speckles: leaves grown in low light come in greener; move it somewhere brighter for new growth.
